Interpretation and limitations

DLP = CTDIvol × scan length when derived from a single representative acquisition. If a scanner-reported DLP is available, use that reported value. Effective dose is approximated as E ≈ DLP × k and is intended for population-level comparisons, not patient-specific radiation risk or absorbed organ dose.

The “Load example” button uses an illustrative adult chest coefficient of 0.014 mSv/(mGy·cm), not a universal factor. Coefficients vary by anatomy, age, scanner technique and phantom assumptions. This tool does not compute SSDE or determine ACR pass/fail; diagnostic reference levels are not patient dose limits.

Reference: AAPM Report 96 — Measurement, Reporting, and Management of Radiation Dose in CT; AAPM Lung Cancer Screening CT Protocols, v6.
